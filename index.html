Window animations · Windows 11 x64

Windows that move like they weigh something.

Drag a window and it follows your hand on a spring. Minimize it and it melts, rolls or burns away. Open an app and it arrives with a transition instead of a flash.

1.0.0 beta Installs under your own profile — no admin rights. The download lands here when it's ready.

What it does

drag
The window trails your cursor on a spring mesh and settles back when you let go. Spring, friction and weight are yours to tune. Release it and it comes home.
39 effects
Minimize and restore animations, from fade and shrink to fire and burn away — including two of ours, Cinder Break and Ashfall. Each has its own speed, end size and end fade, and the whole set is one dropdown.
open / close
A window arrives with a transition instead of a flash, and leaves with one. Off by default: it is the only part that loads into other apps, so it stays your call.
menus
Pop-up menus fade in with a short upward slide, and slide back down as they close.
dudes
A small character perches on the edge of the taskbar, wanders, blinks, and reacts to your windows. 18 packs ship with it, up to four on screen, and one switch turns the lot off.

What it doesn't do

No telemetry. No accounts. No background connections.

motionstoodio does not phone home. The one request it can make is a version check, and only when you press Check for updates — it fetches a small manifest and sends nothing about you or your machine. Window titles are never written to logs: the recovery journal records a handle, a class name and a process id, which is enough to put a window back and not enough to learn what was in it.
